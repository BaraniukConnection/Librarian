Read. Nothing else.

A calm, offline e-book reader for Android. EPUB, FB2 and TXT. No account, no cloud, no tracking: your books stay on your phone.

Design

Made to get out of the way

Librarian belongs to the Oxygen Theme family: green-tinted calm, one mint accent, and nothing on screen that doesn't earn its place.

One place: your library

No bottom bar, no floating button. Search, highlights, add and settings sit in one menu behind the arrow.

Reading without chrome

The page is all text. Tap the centre for controls; they hide again after five seconds.

Colour belongs to content

Covers without a picture and highlights share the same eight Oxygen mark colours.

Quiet motion

Nothing bounces or loops, and nothing moves while you read. Remove animations turns everything into a fade.

Library

Your shelf, at a glance

Pick up where you left off from the Reading now card, which shows the chapter, your progress and the reading time left.

  • Shelves: All, Reading, To read, Finished
  • Sort by recent, title or author; search by title or author
  • Covers drawn from the book, or generated in Oxygen colours

Reading

Typeset like a book

Justified text with real hyphenation in the book's own language. Each chapter is laid out once, so a paragraph that runs onto the next page keeps exactly the same lines.

I am already far north of London, and as I walk in the streets of Petersburgh, I feel a cold northern breeze play upon my cheeks, which braces my nerves and fills me with delight.

  • Contents, bookmarks, search in book and a position slider
  • Literata, Manrope, Atkinson Hyperlegible or the system font, in any size
  • A light or dark page, independent of the app theme
  • Pages that follow your finger, or continuous scroll; no animation for e-ink

Highlights

Keep what matters

Press and hold a sentence, then drag the handles word by word. Pick a colour, add a note, or pass the text to the apps already on your phone.

  • Eight highlight colours, plus notes on any passage
  • Translate, dictionary, copy and find in book
  • All highlights in one place, filtered by book or by notes
  • Export as Markdown through the share sheet

Screens

Light first, dark when you want it

The interface speaks English or Ukrainian, chosen in Settings without changing the phone's language.

Formats

Bring your own books

Each book is copied into the app when you add it, so it keeps working if the original file moves. Open EPUB and FB2 files straight from a file manager or chat app.

EPUB 2 / 3

EPUB

Text, chapter titles from the table of contents, cover, title, author and language.

FB2 · FB2.ZIP

FictionBook

Sections, titles, epigraphs and poems, plus cover, title, author and language.

UTF-8 · CP1251

Plain text

The encoding is detected automatically, and chapters are found from lines like Chapter 5 or Розділ 5.

Open source

Build it yourself

You need JDK 17 or newer and the Android SDK (platform 36).

# debug APK → app/build/outputs/apk/debug/
./gradlew :app:assembleDebug

# parsers, selection, search, progress
./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest

Under the hood

Each chapter is laid out once as a single text layout, and a page is a window onto it. Your place is stored as chapter + character offset, so it survives any font change.
